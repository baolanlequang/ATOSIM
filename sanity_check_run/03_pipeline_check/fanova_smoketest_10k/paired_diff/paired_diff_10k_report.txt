Threshold-appropriateness note:
  0.02/0.05/0.10 are absolute thresholds. For attack_success_rate and
  attack_severity, whose natural ranges are ~[0.15,0.95] and ~[0.3,2.1] respectively,
  these thresholds represent genuinely small-to-moderate perturbations, comparable
  in spirit to the original 1000-row ASR analysis. For conditional_reorg_depth,
  whose ENTIRE observed range is ~[2.0, 2.4] (std ~0.015-0.02), a 0.02 absolute
  threshold is comparable to the outcome's entire standard deviation -- the same
  thresholds are NOT on a comparable relative scale for this outcome. Reported below
  for consistency/comparability across outcomes, but should not be read as 'similarly
  small' across all three.
