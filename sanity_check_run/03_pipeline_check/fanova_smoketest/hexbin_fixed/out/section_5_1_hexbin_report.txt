--- Task 1: raw P vs. 1-P/log decision ---
Decision: raw P, linear Y-axis, NOT 1-P/log.
Reasoning: the earlier log-1-P transform was needed for the BOXPLOT because a box/whisker
summary of a near-constant value has no visible shape unless the residual variation is
stretched out on a log axis. A hexbin does not have that problem - overplotting is handled by
the color/count dimension, not by spreading points along Y. Raw P lets the saturated band show
up honestly as a dense dark region at P~1.0 while sparse low-P outliers still get their own
(lighter) hex cells at their true P value. 1-P/log would in fact be WORSE here: P=1.0 rows
(98% of selfish/lead_stubborn) give 1-P=0, undefined on a log axis, forcing the same exclusion
problem as the boxplot version right back into a plot type that was supposed to avoid needing it.
selfish: n=1000, P range [0.1700, 0.8500], P 1st pct=0.2300
lead_stubborn: n=1000, P range [0.2100, 0.8350], P 1st pct=0.2400
trail_stubborn: n=249500, P range [0.2550, 1.0000], P 1st pct=0.3950

--- Task 2: gridsize ---
gridsize=60 (x-cells across [0.20,0.50] -> ~0.0050 wide per cell), matching the resolution of the earlier binned analysis (~40-42k rows per 0.05-wide alpha region here spread over ~60 bins). mincnt=1 so every non-empty cell is drawn (no cell is silently dropped); log color norm shared across panels (see below) so cell shading is cross-strategy comparable, not just within-panel relative.
